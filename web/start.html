gaia

gaia is a night-shift console for produced water, the salt water that comes up with every barrel of oil. from the sensor readings alone it finds what is failing, like a pump that stops, a pipeline leak or a worn pump. it says how long until a tank forces a shutdown and finds the cheapest fix: trucks, shutting in wells early, or letting it trip. a person always holds to confirm before anything moves. give it a blueprint or photos of a field and it turns them into a 3D model.
