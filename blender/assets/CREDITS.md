each source's own license page or the License.txt inside its download.

## How the licenses were checked

- **Poly Haven:** https://polyhaven.com/license says: 'Our assets are all licensed as CC0' and links to creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/. Files and authors came from the official API (api.polyhaven.com /files and /info), with every download checked against the API md5.
- **ambientCG:** https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/ says: 'All ambientCG assets are provided under the Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal License.' The download link came from the official API (ambientcg.com/api/v2/full_json).
- **Kenney Nature Kit 2.1:** License.txt inside the Nature Kit zip from kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit says: 'License: (Creative Commons Zero, CC0) http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/'
- **Kenney Car Kit 3.1:** License.txt inside the Car Kit zip from kenney.nl/assets/car-kit says: 'License: (Creative Commons Zero, CC0) http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/'
- **Kenney City Kit Industrial 2.0:** License.txt inside the City Kit Industrial zip from kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-industrial says: 'License: (Creative Commons Zero, CC0) http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/'

## Changes we made

Models were converted to GLB in Blender, joined into one mesh each, scaled to meters,
set to rest on Z = 0, and renamed `SCENERY_*`. Some were decimated or had leaf cards thinned
to stay under 15,000 triangles. Leaf alpha maps were merged into the color maps, and the
Kenney grass was recolored to dry straw. Textures were re-encoded as JPEG at 1K (2K for the
main ground). The notes in `manifest.json` list the changes for each asset.

## Gaps

- **Barbed wire ranch fence (posts with wire strands).** No CC0 model on Poly Haven, ambientCG, or Kenney. The kit has a wooden post-and-rail fence instead. Wire strands are easy to generate in the scenery script as thin curves between posts.
- **Yucca and prickly pear.** No CC0 model on Poly Haven, ambientCG, or Kenney. Kenney's cactus is a saguaro shape that does not grow in Texas, so it was left out.
- **Cattle guard (optional).** No CC0 model found on the allowed sources.
- **Pallets and a portable toilet (optional).** No CC0 model found on the allowed sources.
- **Source skipped: Quaternius.** Its packs download through a Google Drive folder, not a direct link, so they were left out under the direct-download, no-login rule.
